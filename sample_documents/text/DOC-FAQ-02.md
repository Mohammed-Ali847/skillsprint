# Customer Support Operations & Refund Handling FAQ
Doc Code: DOC-FAQ-02 | Type: FAQ | Version: 1.0
Department: CUST_OPS | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section FAQ-2.1: Q: How quickly must Priority 1 outages be escalated?
A: Customer support agents typically escalate P1 tickets to engineering within 30 minutes if initial diagnostics fail. (Note: Contradicts DOC-SOP-02 which strictly mandates a 15-minute escalation SLA).

## Section FAQ-2.2: Q: Can support agents issue customer billing refunds?
A: Support executives can approve customer courtesy credits up to $500 if the customer is dissatisfied. (Note: Contradicts DOC-SOP-02 which caps frontline support credits at $150).

## Section FAQ-2.3: Q: Where can I look up customer SLA entitlements?
A: Check the Salesforce Service Cloud Entitlements tab on the account record.
