## Section HDB-1.1: Welcome to ApexNova: Mission and Core Values
Welcome to the team! Our four core pillars are: Relentless Innovation, Radical Transparency, Uncompromising Integrity, and Customer Obsession. We empower every employee to speak up, act with urgency, and foster inclusive teamwork.

## Section HDB-1.2: Core Working Hours & Flexible Schedules
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in your respective hub time zone. Flexible arrangements can be coordinated with your direct manager.

## Section HDB-1.3: Professional Development & Learning Stipend
Full-time employees are eligible for an annual $1,500 USD learning stipend to pursue industry certifications (AWS, CISSP, PMP) and educational conferences.

## Section HDB-1.4: Dress Code & Workplace Presentation
ApexNova maintains a business-casual dress environment for office presence and customer-facing video conferences.
