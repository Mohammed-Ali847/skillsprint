# Customer Ticket Escalation & SLA Management SOP
Doc Code: DOC-SOP-02 | Type: SOP | Version: 2.0
Department: CUST_OPS | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 2.1: Ticket Priority Triage & Response SLAs
Customer tickets are triaged into P1 (Urgent/System Down: 15-minute response SLA), P2 (High/Degraded: 1-hour SLA), P3 (Normal: 4-hour SLA), and P4 (Low: 24-hour SLA).

## Section 2.2: 15-Minute Escalation Threshold for P1
If a P1 customer outage ticket is unresolved after 15 minutes, it must be automatically escalated to Engineering Duty Lead and Customer Success Director.

## Section 2.3: Customer Identity Verification Protocol
Before modifying tenant configurations, releasing diagnostic logs, or resetting MFA, Support Executives must authenticate customer caller identity via cryptographic one-time security PIN.

## Section 2.4: Customer Concession & Refund Authority Limits
Support Executives may authorize billing credits or refunds up to $150 USD. Credits between $150 and $1,000 require Finance Associate review; amounts above $1,000 require VP approval.
