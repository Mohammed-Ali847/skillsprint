# Whistleblower Protection & Non-Retaliation Policy
Doc Code: DOC-POL-10 | Type: POLICY | Version: 1.0
Department: HR_PEOPLE | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 10.1: Scope of Whistleblower Disclosures
Employees are protected when reporting good-faith suspicions of financial fraud, bribery, major safety violations, or regulatory non-compliance.

## Section 10.2: Confidential Ethics Hotline
Anonymous reports can be lodged 24/7 via the independent EthicsPoint hotline (ethics.apexnova.internal) or phone hotlines.

## Section 10.3: Strict Non-Retaliation Guarantee
Retaliation of any kind against an employee making a report in good faith is strictly prohibited and results in immediate dismissal of the retaliator.
