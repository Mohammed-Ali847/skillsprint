# Comprehensive Role Descriptions & Competency Framework Guide
Doc Code: DOC-ROLE-01 | Type: ROLE_DESCRIPTION | Version: 1.0
Department: HR_PEOPLE | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section ROL-1.1: Information Security Analyst Competencies
Must master SIEM analysis (Splunk), threat triage, vulnerability scanning, and incident response SOPs. Responsible for reviewing access tickets and enforcing enterprise security policies.

## Section ROL-1.2: Customer Support Executive Competencies
Must master Zendesk/Salesforce Service Cloud, client SLA verification, identity authentication protocols, and escalation paths for P1 outages.

## Section ROL-1.3: Software Support Engineer Competencies
Must understand Git branch workflows, pull request review requirements, CI/CD pipeline troubleshooting, and zero-defect SAST policy adherence.

## Section ROL-1.4: Data Analyst Competencies
Must master SQL, data masking techniques, secure cloud analytics notebooks, and privacy regulation requirements (GDPR/CCPA).

## Section ROL-1.5: Commercial Sales Executive Competencies
Must master CRM pipeline hygiene, enterprise discount matrix, mutual NDA execution, and anti-bribery gift registry rules.

## Section ROL-1.6: HR Executive Competencies
Must master new hire onboarding lifecycle, compliance training verification, and 1-hour access revocation for departures.

## Section ROL-1.7: Finance Associate Competencies
Must master Concur expense auditing, 14-day receipt compliance, per diem enforcement, and financial approval thresholds.

## Section ROL-1.8: Operations Coordinator Competencies
Must master endpoint imaging, asset tracking in Snipe-IT, full-disk encryption validation, and NIST 800-88 sanitization.

## Section ROL-1.9: Marketing Executive Competencies
Must master customer opt-in consent verification, brand guidelines, and copyright clearance for all corporate public materials.

## Section ROL-1.10: Engineering Team Leader Competencies
Must master agile delivery, dual code review governance, Change Advisory Board (CAB) requests, and quarterly access recertifications.
