## Section FAQ-1.1: Q: How often must I change my password?
A: Passwords expire every 180 days according to legacy helpdesk tips. (Note: This is an informal FAQ clause that contradicts DOC-POL-01 v2.0 which mandates 90-day rotation; policy precedence must resolve in favor of the policy).

## Section FAQ-1.2: Q: Can I use personal SMS for multi-factor authentication?
A: Many employees set up SMS codes on their phone during orientation. (Note: Contradicts DOC-POL-01 v2.0 requiring FIDO2 or app-based authenticator).

## Section FAQ-1.3: Q: What should I do if my screen locks while presenting?
A: You can temporarily disable auto-lock during client meetings for up to 30 minutes using the presentation utility.
