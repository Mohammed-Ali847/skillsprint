# Workplace Health, Physical Safety & Emergency Response Policy
Doc Code: DOC-POL-09 | Type: POLICY | Version: 1.0
Department: INFRA_IT | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 9.1: Physical Access & Visitor Protocol
Employees must wear security badges at all times on company premises. Tailgating into secured data rooms is strictly forbidden. Visitors must register and remain escorted.

## Section 9.2: Emergency Evacuation & Fire Alarms
Upon hearing the fire alarm, all occupants must evacuate via emergency stairs to designated muster points. Do not attempt to use elevators.

## Section 9.3: Accident & Injury Reporting SLA
Any injury, near miss, or hazard on company property must be reported to Facilities and HR within 2 hours of the incident.
