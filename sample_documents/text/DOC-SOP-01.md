# Incident Response, Triage & Breach Notification SOP
Doc Code: DOC-SOP-01 | Type: SOP | Version: 2.0
Department: CYBER_SEC | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 1.1: Incident Severity Classification
Incidents are classified from Severity 1 (Critical data breach or total platform outage) to Severity 4 (Minor cosmetic bug). Sev-1 requires immediate incident commander assignment.

## Section 1.2: 4-Hour Critical Breach Notification SLA
Upon confirmation of a Severity 1 cybersecurity breach involving customer records, the Incident Commander must notify the CISO and Data Protection Officer within 4 hours.

## Section 1.3: Evidence Preservation & Chain of Custody
Memory dumps, system audit logs, and network capture packets must be cryptographically hashed and copied to isolated forensic storage with documented chain of custody.

## Section 1.4: Post-Incident Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
A blameless Post-Mortem and formal RCA report must be published within 5 business days of incident resolution.
