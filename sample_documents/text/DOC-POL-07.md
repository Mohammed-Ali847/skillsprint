# Employee Leave, Attendance & Overtime Policy
Doc Code: DOC-POL-07 | Type: POLICY | Version: 2.0
Department: HR_PEOPLE | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 7.1: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual vacation leave accrued monthly. Leave must be scheduled through the HR portal with manager sign-off.

## Section 7.2: Vacation Carryover Cap & March 31 Deadline
A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year with written manager approval. Carried days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited.

## Section 7.3: Quarterly Mental Health Wellness Days
All employees receive 1 designated paid Wellness Day per quarter (4 per year) that does not reduce accrued vacation balance.

## Section 7.4: Overtime Authorization Standard
Overtime for non-exempt staff must be pre-authorized in writing by Department Managers before being logged in the payroll system.
