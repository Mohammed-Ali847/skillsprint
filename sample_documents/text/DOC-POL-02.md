# Global Data Privacy & Customer Data Handling Policy
Doc Code: DOC-POL-02 | Type: POLICY | Version: 2.0
Department: DATA_AI | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 2.1: Privacy Framework & GDPR/CCPA Compliance
ApexNova complies with GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards. Personal Identifiable Information (PII) must only be collected for explicitly consented business purposes.

## Section 2.2: Strict Ban on Real PII in Non-Production
Under no circumstances may live production customer data or unmasked PII be imported or stored in development, staging, sandbox, or test environments. Fully synthetic or tokenized data must be utilized.

## Section 2.3: Right to Be Forgotten & Erasure SLA
Customer requests for data erasure must be processed and fully wiped from all operational clusters and cold backups within 30 calendar days.

## Section 2.4: Data Residency and Regional Isolation
Customer telemetry and database records must remain within the sovereign region (EU, US, APAC) designated in the customer agreement. Cross-border transfers require legal DPA approval.

## Section 2.5: Data Retention & Archival Schedules
Transactional logs must be archived after 90 days and permanently expunged after 365 days unless legal holds apply.
