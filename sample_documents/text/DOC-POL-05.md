# Code of Business Conduct, Ethics & Anti-Bribery Policy
Doc Code: DOC-POL-05 | Type: POLICY | Version: 1.0
Department: HR_PEOPLE | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 5.1: Workplace Respect & Harassment Prevention
ApexNova is committed to a harassment-free environment. Discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age is prohibited.

## Section 5.2: Anti-Bribery & FCPA Compliance
Employees must not offer, pay, solicit, or accept bribes, kickbacks, or facilitation payments. Gifts or hospitality to or from clients exceeding $50 USD in value must be formally declared in the Compliance Gift Registry.

## Section 5.3: Conflict of Interest Disclosures
Employees must disclose outside commercial interests, advisory roles, or family relationships with active vendors using the annual Ethics Declaration portal within 14 days of employment.

## Section 5.4: Fair Competition & Antitrust Rules
Personnel must never participate in price-fixing, market division, or unlawful competitor discussions.
