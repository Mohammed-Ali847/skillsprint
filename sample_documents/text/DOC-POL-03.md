# Access Control, Identity & Credential Management Policy
Doc Code: DOC-POL-03 | Type: POLICY | Version: 2.0
Department: CYBER_SEC | Organization: ApexNova Global Technologies

## Section 3.1: Principle of Least Privilege & RBAC
Access to corporate and production infrastructure is governed strictly by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Employees receive only minimum permissions necessary for their role.

## Section 3.2: Just-In-Time (JIT) Privileged Access
Permanent root or cluster admin credentials are prohibited. Privileged access to production environments requires dual-authorization ticketing and expires automatically after 8 hours.

## Section 3.3: Quarterly Access Recertification
Managers must recertify employee access permissions every 90 days. Accounts inactive for over 45 days are automatically suspended.

## Section 3.4: Credential Sharing Prohibition
Sharing user accounts, passwords, or personal SSH keys is strictly prohibited and results in immediate termination of employment.
